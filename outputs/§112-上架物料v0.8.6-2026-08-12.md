### 1.3 Description (英文, 4000 字内)

```
SpeakMirror AI is a 100% offline meeting assistant. All recording, 
transcription, summarization, and LLM inference run entirely on your Mac.

No account. No registration. No internet required — install and use.

=== KEY FEATURES ===

• Real-time Speech-to-Text: Bilingual (Chinese/English), 8 local models,
  95%+ accuracy on Mandarin.
• Smart Summaries: 8 meeting templates + 3 vertical domains 
  (Legal / Medical / General).
• Cross-Meeting Knowledge Graph: Auto-detects recurring topics, builds
  long-term dossiers.
• Action Items Tracking: Auto-extracts TODOs, supports checkboxes,
  cross-meeting aggregation.
• Obsidian Two-Way Sync: Notes auto-flow into your Obsidian vault.
• MCP Server: Lets Claude / Cursor / AI assistants query your 
  meeting archive.
• Real-time Q&A (⌥+Space): Mid-meeting questions, 3 suggestions in 3s.
• Offline LLM: Built-in Qwen 3.5 2B inference engine, no cloud needed.

=== 8 LOCAL ASR MODELS (sorted by accuracy) ===

1. FunASR-Nano 947MB (Pro, experimental high-precision)
2. SenseVoice 228MB (high-precision, multi-language)
3. Paraformer-zh 216MB (fallback, stable for 1h+ audio)
4. Whisper Tiny/Base/Small/Medium (built-in bilingual)
5. Parakeet (Apple Silicon optimized)

=== 8 HOTWORD PACKS ===

• General IT (300 words)
• Legal (538 + 800 curated from Sogou)
• Medical (488 + 800 curated from Sogou)
• Finance (176 words)

=== PRIVACY PROMISE ===

SpeakMirror AI's core philosophy is "Your data belongs to you":
• All model weights loaded locally, never uploaded
• No account signup, no cloud sync
• No telemetry, no Crashlytics
• Recording/transcripts/summaries stay on your disk, you can delete anytime
• We can't see your data because it never leaves your device

=== WHO IS IT FOR ===

• Lawyers / doctors / consultants (vertical templates + hotwords)
• Product managers / designers (cross-meeting knowledge graph)
• Founders / indie workers (local, no business secrets leak)
• Academic researchers (Obsidian sync, long-term archive)
• Privacy-conscious users (zero cloud, zero account)

=== SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS ===

• macOS 12.0+
• Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4 recommended
• 8GB RAM minimum, 16GB recommended (LLM inference)
• 5GB disk space (model weights)

=== DISCLAIMER ===

All features run offline. The developer cannot access any user data.
Please comply with local laws when using this application.
```
